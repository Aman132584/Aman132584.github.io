I'm a CS student focused on building systems that don't just process data — they understand, reason, and take action. My core work lives at the frontier of Agentic AI, where I design autonomous pipelines that use LLMs as reasoning engines, chain tools together, and solve multi-step problems without hand-holding.

I work extensively with Large Language Models via API — integrating GPT, Claude, and Gemini into real applications through prompt engineering, structured outputs, and function calling. Alongside that, I build RAG pipelines that ground models in real knowledge, cutting hallucination and making responses actually trustworthy.

I also bring solid fundamentals — Neural Networks for vision tasks and classical Machine Learning models for the problems that don't need a language model to solve them.
